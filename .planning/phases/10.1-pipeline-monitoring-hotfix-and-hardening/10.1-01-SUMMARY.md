## Evidence: Pre-migration created_at Format Histogram

From live `data/vna_alerts.db` (438 alerts total):

```
aware_T  | naive_T | space_micro | space_sec
---------|---------|-------------|----------
238      | 200     | 0           | 0
```

- **238 aware T rows**: written by `notifier._save_to_db` via `datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat()` → `'2026-04-23T21:42:50+00:00'`
- **200 naive T rows**: written by `operations.create_alert` via `datetime.utcnow().isoformat()` → `'2026-04-23T21:42:50.142222'`
- **0 space-sep rows**: no rows with space separator yet (migration 004 will normalize all to space-sep)
- **Root cause confirmed**: `+` (ASCII 43) in aware rows sorts below digits (`2` = ASCII 50), breaking `MAX(created_at)` across the mixed corpus
